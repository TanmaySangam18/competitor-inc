## 0 · The honest odds (we hold ourselves to our own standard)

$10K MRR in ~30 days from $0 and no existing audience is **top-decile hard** — most bootstrapped SaaS
take 6–18 months. We say so out loud because the brand is honesty. This plan **maximizes the odds**; it
does not pretend they're high. Decision rule (Levels default-alive):

- **Win:** ≥ $10K MRR by end of month 2 → post the success story.
- **Strong signal:** $3–5K MRR + healthy activation/retention → *keep going*, do not kill on a near-miss.
- **Kill/pivot:** < ~$1.5K MRR **and** flat week-over-week with no working channel → stop, reassess.

The point of launching is to **learn the real conversion + the one channel that works**. Revenue is the
scoreboard; the channel is the prize.

## 2 · The math — backsolve from $10K (no hand-waving)

Frozen launch pricing (already live in the app): **Validate $0 · Operator $39/mo · Founding $99 once
(~150 seats, capped, launch-only).**

| Lever | What it is | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator $39/mo** | the recurring product | **MRR** |
| **Founding $99 once** | lifetime Operator, ~150 capped seats | **one-time cash / runway — NOT MRR** |

**True-MRR target:** $10,000 ÷ $39 ≈ **257 paying Operators**. That's the honest number, and it's the hard
part. So the engine runs **two tracks at once**:

1. **Cash + proof track (launch spike):** sell out **~150 Founding seats × $99 = ~$15K one-time**. This is
   runway and a loud demand signal — *not* recurring — but every Founding member is a lifetime power user
   who fuels referrals, testimonials, and the success story.
2. **MRR track (the real goal):** convert the launch wave + the one working channel into **$39 Operators**,
   climbing toward 257. Target ramp: **~60–90 Operators (~$3K MRR) by end of month 1 → ~257 (~$10K) by end
   of month 2.**

**Funnel to hit ~250 Operators (illustrative, instrument the real rates in week 1):**

| Stage (AARRR) | Assumption | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** — qualified visitors | — | **~20,000** over 6 weeks |
| **Activation** — sign up + run 1 validation | ~12% of visitors | ~2,400 signups |
| **Revenue** — signup → paid Operator | ~10% | **~240 Operators** |
| **Referral** — each happy user brings | ~0.3 more | amplifies the top of funnel |
| **Retention** — keep them (low churn) | < 6%/mo | protects the MRR base |

These rates are guesses on purpose — **week 1's job is to replace every number here with a measured one.**
The biggest unknown and the biggest lever is **signup → paid**; a move from 10% → 15% is the difference
between $7K and $10K. Conversion + retention beat raw traffic.

**Post-Monday accelerators (need the friend + your approval — flagged, not built):** an **annual prepay**
(e.g. $290/yr, pulls MRR forward + cuts churn), a **Studio $99/mo** tier for multi-company power users (the
"$10K workhorse" in the revenue doc — 100 × $99 = $10K with *far fewer* accounts), and **$29 validation
packs**. Any of these makes the math dramatically easier; all are pricing changes, so they wait for Monday.

## 4 · The engine — how the crew actually operates (Hacking Growth cadence)

1. **North Star metric:** **activated-and-paying Operators** (a customer who ran a real validation *and* is
   on $39/mo). It captures value delivered *and* value captured — not just signups.
2. **One Metric That Matters, by stage:** pre-launch = waitlist signups; launch week = signup→paid %;
   weeks 1–4 = net-new MRR/week. Everyone rallies to the *current* OMTM; Quant kills vanity metrics.
3. **AARRR ownership:** every stage in §3 has exactly one owning agent — no orphaned funnel steps.
4. **High-tempo testing (the core loop, weekly):**
   `ideate → score by ICE (Impact × Confidence × Ease) → ship the top 2–3 → measure → keep/kill → log learning.`
   Velocity of *learning* is the real growth lever, not any single tactic.
5. **Bullseye channels (weeks 1–2):** test ~5 cheap channels in parallel —
   **(a)** launch platforms (HN / Product Hunt), **(b)** beachhead communities (IndieHackers, r/SaaS, X
   build-community), **(c)** honest comparison **SEO**, **(d)** founder 1:1 outreach/DMs, **(e)** one small
   **paid** test (only with your approval + a hard cap). Then **kill 3, double down on the 1–2 that convert.**
   One channel that works beats five that sort-of do.
6. **Spend discipline (our own money model):** real spend = gated, capped, approval-first. We absorb our own
   failed-work compute via cheap/BYOK models, so a dud experiment costs us ≈ $0 in product terms — the only
   real cost is any ad budget *you* approve. Default to free channels; earn the right to pay.

## 5 · The 6-week cadence (launch → $10K verdict)

**Pre-launch — now → Mon 2026-06-22 handoff (Tanmay + friend):**
friend deploys to Vercel + buys domain + flips on Stripe/Resend/Supabase per [launch/runbook.md](../launch/runbook.md);
waitlist + capped Founding teaser live; **all launch assets written and queued** (Pitch); funnel
instrumented (Quant). *No public posts yet — surprise launch.*

**Week 0 — Launch (the spike, Pitch leads):** big-bang same-day drop — Show HN + Product Hunt + the X thread
+ email the waitlist — all pointing at the **0% cut / proof-first** wedge and the **capped Founding seats**
(Superhuman-style scarcity). Echo seeds the beachhead communities *with help, not spam*. **Goal: sell out
Founding (~$15K cash) + a first cohort of Operators.**

**Weeks 1–2 — Find the channel (Surge leads):** run the Bullseye 5-channel test; Quant reports CAC +
signup→paid per channel; Guard drives activation so the traffic actually converts; Quill ships the
comparison page. **Exit criteria: one channel with a repeatable, affordable signup→paid.**

**Weeks 3–4 — Double down + convert (Ledger leads):** pour effort into the winning channel; Ledger runs
trial→paid nudges and pricing clarity; Guard attacks churn; Surge turns happy Founding members into the
referral loop. **Target: cross ~$3K → push toward $10K MRR.**

**End of month 2 — Verdict (Apex):** read MRR against §0. **≥ $10K → ship the success story.** Near-miss →
keep going. Flat-and-low → pivot the channel or the offer.
