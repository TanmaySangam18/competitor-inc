# Sales pack v1 — arm the first sale ("AI growth desk" → indie hackers)

Turnkey kit so the founder can start selling today. Offer + reasoning: [[charter-and-goal]], docs/OFFER-v1.md.
All copy is honest — no invented stats, no fake social proof, no spam. Personalize every send.

## The pitch (DM / one-paragraph version)
> I run an AI crew that does the weekly growth work solo founders hate — content, SEO, outreach drafts, and a
> measured experiment — shipped every week, with receipts, and you approve everything before it goes out. It's
> $199/mo, or a $99 one-week trial with real deliverables so you can judge it before committing. Want me to run
> a free 3-point growth audit on [their product] so you can see how I think?

**Lead with the free audit** (the `/score`-style hook) → deliver a real, useful mini-audit → convert to the
$99 trial → convert to $199/mo. Value before the ask (matches your funnel playbook).

## Channel 1 — Reddit (r/SaaS, r/indiehackers) — help-first, one sub at a time
**Title:** I'll run a free 3-point growth audit on your SaaS (roast + fixes)
> I build AI tooling for founders and I'm sharpening a "growth desk" service. To pressure-test it I'll do a
> free 3-point growth audit on the first ~10 SaaS that comment — your riskiest growth gap, one content angle,
> and one experiment to run this week. Drop your URL + who it's for. No pitch unless you ask.

Reply to every comment with a genuinely useful audit. If they ask "can you just do this for me?" → the trial.

## Channel 2 — X / Indie Hackers
- Build-in-public: post the audits you do (with permission) as mini case-threads — "here's the growth gap I
  found on [X] and the fix." Ends with: "I do this weekly as a service — DM 'desk' if you want it."
- DM (to founders who engage): the pitch paragraph above, personalized to their product.

## Channel 3 — Direct email (CAN-SPAM: real name, address, one-click opt-out)
**Subject:** quick growth idea for [product]
> Hi [name] — I saw [specific thing about their product]. One gap I'd fix first: [one real, specific idea].
> I run an AI growth desk that ships this kind of work weekly (you approve everything). Happy to do a free
> 3-point audit if useful — no obligation. — Tanmay · [address] · reply "stop" to opt out.

One personalized follow-up after 4–5 days, then stop (Rule 4: 14 days silent = dead).

## Finding the first ~10 targets (warm/public, no scraping, no spam)
- r/SaaS + r/indiehackers "share your project" threads; IndieHackers "milestones"; X #buildinpublic.
- Anyone who already posts about struggling with marketing/distribution = perfect (weekly pain, Rule 3).
- Qualify: has a live product, is solo/small, is *already spending* on tools/ads/freelancers (Rule 1).

## Collect path (founder sets up — I can't touch payments)
1. In Polar, create two products: **Growth desk $199/mo (recurring)** and **Trial run $99 (one-time)**.
2. Paste their checkout links here as env vars (the /join tiers already read them). Or just send the Polar
   link 1:1 to a founder who says yes — that's the fastest first dollar.
3. **Collected = settled.** It counts only when Polar shows the money cleared (charter). I never mark revenue
   from a promise.

## What the crew delivers each week (so you can promise it truthfully)
- 2 content/SEO pieces (drafted, ready to publish)
- 1 outreach batch (drafted to their list; they send)
- 1 growth experiment proposed + measured against their real funnel
- A weekly report: what shipped · the numbers · honest verdict · next move (receipts on everything)

Sell only this. If the model can't do a piece well yet, we say so and don't bill for it — honest verdict,
every time.
