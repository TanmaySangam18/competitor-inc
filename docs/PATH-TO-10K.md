# Path to $10K — how the founder gets paid first

The blunt goal: **money in the founder's pocket → $10K MRR → then scale customers' money.** This is the action
plan, grounded in books: **Hormozi — _$100M Offers_ & _$100M Leads_** (irresistible offer + lead engine) ·
**Walling — _The SaaS Playbook_** (charge more, niche that pays) · **Fitzpatrick — _The Mom Test_** (convert on
commitment) · **Graham — _Do Things That Don't Scale_** (win the first customers by hand).

## The honest premise (no magic)
- **Recurring path:** ~**34 × Founder ($299/mo)** = $10K MRR. Or a mix: 20 Founder ($6K) + a dozen Operator
  ($39) + Sprints. There is no version where this happens without the founder **personally selling the first
  ~20–30** (Do Things That Don't Scale). Tools can't shortcut the first dollars.
- **Survival cash NOW:** one-time **Validation Sprints ($499)** put money in this week while MRR builds. Sell
  3–4 Sprints = ~$2K runway in days; they upsell into Founder.
- **Timeline, honest:** first paid Sprint in days, first Founder within ~2 weeks, $10K MRR is a multi-week grind
  — measured weekly on the KPI board, not promised.

## 1. The Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi) — the thing that gets you paid
Make saying no feel dumb. Put this on `/join` (the Founder tier) and lead every conversation with it:
- **Dream outcome:** "A validated, built, launched company — without wasting months or your savings on
  something nobody wants."
- **Value stack:** done-with-you validation (commitment-based) + the MVP built + launch + the playbook library +
  a weekly working session + the Glass Box + own-everything / 0% revenue share.
- **Risk reversal (on-brand = our wedge):** *The Honest Guarantee* — "In your first 2 weeks we hand you a clear
  go/no-go backed by **real commitment signals** (a pre-order, deposits, signed intent) — or you don't pay that
  month." We can promise this because validation, not the build, is the deliverable.
- **Scarcity (real):** "We take only a handful of founders at a time" (true — founder bandwidth).
- **Urgency:** founding-cohort price locks now; it goes up after the first cohort.
- **Price anchor:** vs a $5–15k developer or 3 wasted months, $299/mo is a rounding error. Say that out loud.

**Sprint offer (cash now):** "**$499 Validation Sprint** — in 7 days you'll know if it's worth building, with
real commitment evidence in hand, or your money back. Credits 100% toward Founder if you continue."

## 2. Lead gen — Hormozi's Core Four, prioritized for $0 budget
1. **WARM outreach (do this first).** List 30–50 people you already know who are building / want to build / have
   a painful problem. Reach out 1:1, give value first (a free validation read), *then* offer. Highest conversion,
   zero cost. (Mom Test on every call.)
2. **Polsia refugees (warm-ish, ethical).** They've *felt* build-without-validate pain. Meet them where they
   already complain — Trustpilot reviews, Reddit, "Polsia alternative" searches — with genuine help, never a
   link-drop in Polsia. "Got burned building something nobody wanted? That's the thing we prevent."
3. **CONTENT (founder-led short-form).** Copy Result's GTM: you on camera, the honesty angle ("I'm building an AI
   that tells you NOT to build it"). Post-launch: the "I asked the $250M AI to build my startup — it skipped
   validation" demo. Compounding + free.
4. **COLD outreach + PAID ads = HOLD** (per the Lead Desk decision + no budget). Revisit after first paying users.
