# Growth playbook — Build in Public (the Surge agent)

## What Polsia actually did (researched June 2026)

Beyond the product, Polsia's *distribution* is the real engine. From public sources:

- **A live transparency dashboard.** Ben Cera built a real-time board (`polsia.com/live`) showing
  customer growth + metrics, and tweeted it. This flipped fundraising from *"here's my deck"* to
  *"here's the proof happening in real time."* Transparency became the distribution.
- **An AI Growth Agent that builds in public.** It scans the internet for "infrastructure blowups"
  and trending solopreneur topics, then **auto-drafts "Build in Public" updates**. The companies it
  builds tweet about themselves — names, mission statements, "shipped 4 tasks, 2 emails, posted to
  Twitter in the last 60 minutes."
- **Transparency as defense against "AI slop."** Being radically open is how they counter the
  skepticism that AI-built companies are low-quality.
- (Revenue: a flat ~$49/mo to cover compute, with the real money on a **20% revenue share**.)

Sources: GTMnow / The GTM Newsletter (Ben Cera interview), Medium (Zack Liu, "5-Agent Swarm"),
ain.ua funding report, and posts on X. *Internal research only — we do not position against them in
product copy.*

## The playbook: **Build in Public** (Levels / Kahl / Kleon "Show Your Work")

Ship your metrics, your wins, and your *misses* in the open, continuously. It compounds trust and
distribution at ~$0 cost. competitor.inc is structurally built for this — we already have the asset
Polsia had to bolt on: **the `/live` Glass Box board.**

## Why we can do it *better* (counter-positioned)

Polsia posts about *what it built*. We can post about **what we validated** — including the kills.
"We ran a real demand test on X. The signal was weak. We told the founder not to build it." That is
*more* credible content than "look what we shipped," and it's on-brand (honesty is the product).

## How it maps to our crew

**Surge (Growth) runs this.** Its playbook is *Hacking Growth* (Sean Ellis) **+ Build in Public**:
1. Watches the Glass Box for milestone moments (a strong verdict, an honest kill, an MVP shipping, a
   revenue tick).
2. Drafts a "build/learn in public" post from that proof — **queued to the Approval Inbox**, never
   auto-sent (outreach is a one-way door).
3. You approve → it posts. The `/live` board is the permanent, public receipt.

## The synthesis with our launch (resolving the apparent conflict)

We earlier chose a **big-bang surprise launch**, not build-in-public. Both are right, in sequence:

- **Launch moment = big bang.** Surprise drop + Show HN / PH / X + capped Founding scarcity. One loud
  day.
- **After launch = build in public, forever.** The `/live` board + Surge's drafted posts become the
  always-on growth engine. The big bang earns the first audience; building in public compounds it.

> Recommendation: **yes, adopt it — but as the post-launch growth motion, powered by Surge + `/live`,
> and leaning on honest "we killed this one" posts as our signature.**
