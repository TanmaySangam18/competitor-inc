# Polsia Decode — Now / Next / Our Plays (June 2026)

> Strategic read on Polsia to inform competitor.inc's direction. Grounded in sourced facts; all

## A. What Polsia is doing NOW (decoded)

**The product:** $49/mo agent-orchestration OS that auto-builds site + DB + email + marketing +
ad accounts and runs them 24/7 via specialized agents. Proactive nightly loop ("works while you sleep").

**The money (this is the key tell):** ~$10M ARR splits as **~$4.6M subscriptions + ~$2M task
packages + ~$2M advertising**. New capital is explicitly going to **(1) AI compute and (2) marketing**,
and Cera has said **advertising — not subscriptions — is the intended future revenue driver.**
→ **Decode:** Polsia is quietly becoming an **ad-spend business**. The platform that decides to
run your ads also profits from the ads running. That's an incentive to *spend*, not to *validate*.

**Traction:** ~7,600 paying customers, ~120k projects generated / ~8,500 active, ~+25% WoW. $30M @
$250M from Sound Ventures + True Ventures. Solo founder, zero employees.

**The growth engine = the narrative.** Build-in-public + escalating spectacle: the AI "raised its
own money," the controversial name, and now the frontier move (below). The story drives signups
more than the product does. **Reliability is not the strategy** (Trustpilot ~2.1★; reviews cite
fake "done," no validation, weak support, lock-in).

## B. What they WILL / MIGHT do next

**Stated by the founder (high confidence):**
- **Give the AI 10% equity** and push toward **full autonomy** — the AI **controls profits and
  takes real financial actions** with money it generates.
- Stand up a **"Polsia Foundation" with full-time human hires who operate at the will of the AI.**
  → The inversion: humans serve the AI. Maximal-autonomy frontier play (also maximal PR).
- Pour capital into **compute + marketing**; lean into **ads as the core business model.**

**Inference (reasoned, unconfirmed):**
- **Ad-arbitrage at scale** — take margin/markup on managed ad spend; the more they spend on your
  behalf, the more they make. Expect spend to be encouraged, validation de-emphasized.
- **Up-market / enterprise** — they already list Enterprise/Gov org types; capital → multi-seat,
  SSO, compliance for higher ACV (Gartner: ~40% of enterprise apps to embed agents in 2026).
- **Marketplace / platform** — agent/template marketplace, "Polsia for Platforms" multi-tenant.
- **Deeper real-world transaction** — payments, more ad networks, real infra: from "builds" → "transacts."
- **Lock-in for defensibility** — proprietary hosting/orchestration + data network effects.
- **What they likely WON'T fix soon:** trust/quality/validation/support. Their flywheel is
  autonomy + ads + spectacle, not the skeptical paying user. **That gap is durable.**

## D. What WE (competitor.inc) can do — the plays

**1. Attack the incentive conflict (sharpest wedge).**
Polsia increasingly *profits when you spend* (ad margin). competitor.inc profits only when you *succeed*:
flat fee, **no ad rake**, **validation-first** (won't spend your budget until demand is proven),
auto-refund on failed work. Tagline energy: *"We make money when you do — not when you spend."*
This is a clean, defensible contrast to an ad-arbitrage model.

**2. Take the opposite philosophical pole.**
They're going *"AI controls the capital; humans serve the AI"* (Polsia Foundation). We go
human-first: **"You're still the founder. You own the equity, the data, the decisions — Roomie is
your companion, not your boss."** Full export, no lock-in. Ownable narrative they *can't* copy
without abandoning their own story.

**3. Win a beachhead ICP instead of fighting generalists on breadth.**
Pick one: solo founders / indie hackers validating side-projects (our validation-first flow is
tailor-made), or agencies, or a specific vertical. Tailored agents + templates > "any company."

**4. Make trust the moat, visibly.**
Glass Box + proof-of-work + auto-refund + honest "kill it" reality-checks — and *market* them.
This is exactly where the leaders structurally won't go (it slows the spectacle). Their 2.1★ is our ad copy.

**5. Real creative output, framed our way (Higgsfield).**
Wire Higgsfield so **Pitch generates real ad creative/video** — but *after* validation, not to burn
budget. Closes a parity gap (Polsia's "video generation") and is a visible "it really does the work" proof.

**6. Don't out-spectacle a funded incumbent — out-trust them and own honest distribution.**
We can't win a narrative arms race vs $30M. We win the segment that's *skeptical of the narrative*.

---

### Bottom line
Polsia is racing toward maximal autonomy + an ads-driven model + viral spectacle, and is
deliberately leaving trust, validation, and user-control on the table. The crowd is copying the
*surface* (autonomy, live feeds, even approval gates). The open, defensible position is the
**honest, outcome-aligned, human-in-control operator for a specific audience** — and to *say it
loudly*. Build there; don't chase parity for its own sake.
