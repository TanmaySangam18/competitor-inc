# Content plan — the short-form distribution engine

> Reverse-engineered from how Result/Polsia actually got *reach* (founder-led short-form + a shareable
> gimmick + curated proof). Our shareable gimmick isn't "Tinder for posts" — it's **the honesty reveal**:
> *an AI co-founder that tells you the truth.* These are **launch-week + post-launch** reels (IG / TikTok /
> X / YouTube Shorts / LinkedIn) — NOT pre-launch drip (surprise-launch rule holds; deploy at launch). Most
> footage is repurposed from the [trailer](trailer-storyboard.md) + screen-records of the product.

**ICP framing:** every hook is aimed at the **first-time / student & new-grad founder** — the person
terrified of pouring their savings into an idea nobody wants. Speak to *that* person's fear and relief.

**Format for each:** 15–30s vertical. **Hook in the first 3 seconds** (that's 80% of the battle). End on the
same CTA: *"competitor.inc — prove it before you build it. Link in bio."*

## The engine (how to run it)
- **Cadence:** launch week = 1–2/day (front-load #1, #2, #5, #9); then **3–5/week**. Consistency > polish.
- **One core idea, many cuts:** #1 (the "it told me no" honesty reveal) is your flagship — make 3–4 variants; it's the most counter-intuitive and thus most shareable.
- **Founder-led > brand:** post from *your* face/voice (#6, #8, #9). People follow founders, not logos — that's how Result/Polsia did it.
- **Repurpose:** every reel is a clip of the trailer or a 30-sec screen-record. Make once, cut many.
- **Always-on CTA:** link-in-bio → waitlist / Founding seats (already built).
- **Honesty firewall:** #7 and #10 reference roadmap/vision — keep them clearly framed ("coming soon" / "here's the plan") so we stay honest by design.

**Distribution = (founder short-form) × (the honesty hook) × (consistency) × (a credibility anchor).** This is the machine; the honesty angle is the fuel only *we* have.
