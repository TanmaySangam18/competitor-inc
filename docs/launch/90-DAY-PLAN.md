# The 90-day plan (the Groq-key clock: ~Jun 27 → Aug 27)

> The founder's framing: a self-imposed 90-day clock. **~Days 1–35:** hit a signup target using the agents.
> **~Days 36–90:** use the agents to build *half* of at least one real user's company — deliver enough value
> that they'll **pay for the rest** (first revenue). This doc reads that through the playbooks and makes it
> measurable + honest.

## The playbook lens (what you actually described)
You described a **"do things that don't scale → deliver a slice → charge for the rest"** motion:
- **Paul Graham, "Do things that don't scale"** + **Sam Blond, "demand is the bottleneck / founder-led sales"** → Phase 1: *you* (agent-scaled) hand-recruit the first cohort. Demand is the constraint, not the product.
- **Concierge MVP / paid pilot** → Phase 2: build a real, working *half* for a few founders, prove value, then convert that delivered value into payment. (Charging for *delivered* value — the opposite of Polsia's 1.8★ "charged for vapor.")
- Our own **validate → build → operate** + **loop-engineering "define 'done' measurably"** → set the targets up front.

## Phase 1 — Demand (Days 1–35)
**Goal: prove a channel + a real first cohort. Demand is the bottleneck.**
- **Pick ONE channel** (Bullseye — don't spray). Best fit for the ICP (first-time/student founders + your Boston/Northeastern base): **campus + founder communities** (r/startups, Indie Hackers, founder Discords/Slacks) **+ the honesty-reveal short-form** ("an AI that tells you *not* to build it").
- **Who does what:** *agents* draft content/SEO + Bluesky posts (approval-gated) + run the referral loop; **you** do the irreducible **trust-spark** — cold DMs, posting, campus conversations. Agents can't authentically cold-start (spam/bans); you carry the spark, they scale the rest (~85/15).
- **Targets (grounded in GROWTH-MODEL.md, not vanity):**
  | | Conservative | Expected | Aggressive |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Signups (35d, 1 channel, $0) | ~75 | **~150** | ~400 (a post/reel hits) |
  - **The metric that matters more than raw signups:** **activation ≥40%** (signed up → ran a validation) + **one channel with ~$0 CAC that works** + a **Sean-Ellis read** once ~30–40 have used it (≥40% "very disappointed" = real signal).
- **Why not "2,000"?** That's the big-launch number (25–45% likely *ever*). For a 35-day cold start, 150 *engaged* signups + a working channel is the honest, useful target. Quality of the cohort > the count.

## Phase 2 — Deliver & Charge (Days 36–90)
**Goal: build *half* a real company for a few → convert to first paying customers.**
- From the activated cohort, **pick the 1–3 most engaged first-time founders** (highest intent). Keep it tiny and deep (concierge).
- **Run them through the crew:** real demand test → if it passes → start the build → ship a working **half** (a live landing/MVP slice + first real results). You + agents, founder-led; consequential moves stay approval-gated.
- **Then charge for the rest:** once they're holding a validated, half-built, *working* thing, convert — Operator **$39/mo** or a founding/pilot price. "Charge the rest" = they pay to finish/operate. **Charge for delivered value, never vapor.**
- **Target: 1–3 paying customers (first real revenue) by Day 90.** Explicitly **NOT $10K MRR** — that's top-decile and a *later* goal. First paid dollar from a first-time founder is the real 90-day win.
- **Free-tier fit:** the Groq free limits cap how much real AI you can serve — which is *perfect* for "a few, deep." Don't mass-launch the AI; meter it to the cohort you're converting.

## What to watch (on /house/board)
Phase 1: signups · **activation %** · channel CAC (find the ~$0 one) · Sean-Ellis %.
Phase 2: # in active build · **first $ collected** · the leverage ratio (what % the agents carried).

## Honest risks / why-not
- **The agents won't get the signups alone** — Phase 1 is founder-led, agent-scaled. Budget your own time for the spark.
- **"Half a company in 55 days" only holds for a *simple* first idea** — pick a simple, reachable founder, not a moonshot.
- **Don't charge for vapor** — the moment you over-promise, you become the 1.8★ incumbent. Charge once value is real.
- ⚠️ **The key-expiry is a motivational deadline, not an infra setting.** A Groq key that expires Aug 27 will **silently break real AI on day 90** mid-traction. Set a *calendar* reminder for the deadline, but plan to **rotate the key** (or remove the expiry) so your product doesn't punish your users when the clock hits.

## The honest verdict
35 days to ~150 engaged signups + a working channel: **achievable** with founder-led effort. 90 days to **1–3 paying customers** by delivering real value: **achievable**. 90 days to $10K MRR: **don't anchor on it** (10–20% even by some readings). Win the *first paying customer*; that's what unlocks the raise, the credits, and the story.
