# Plan — beachhead niche + sharpened positioning + surprise launch

> **Playbooks:** **Crossing the Chasm** (Moore — win ONE narrow beachhead first) · **Walling** (a niche
> with money + a reachable channel) · **Dunford "Obviously Awesome"** (position against the real
> alternatives) · the **big-bang surprise-launch** playbook (no build-in-public) · **Levels**
> (validate, default-alive). Built on [`COMPETITIVE-landscape.md`](COMPETITIVE-landscape.md): the
> category is crowded + funded; we win on **trust + a niche + distribution**, not features.

---

## 1 · The beachhead (commit to ONE)

**Primary: multi-bet indie operators who've been burned by — or distrust — autonomous AI builders.**

The ICP, precisely:
- **Who:** solo / 2-person builders who launch **several products a year** — indie hackers with a
  portfolio, micro-agencies, "house of brands" solo operators. Technical-ish but time-poor.
- **The pain (must-have, not nice-to-have):**
  1. They keep **building things nobody wants** — weeks/months burned per dud.
  2. They **tried or fear the autonomous tools** (Polsia/NanoCorp/HeyBoss) and got **over-promise**
     ("marked complete without deploying," "credits burned").
  3. They **resent the revenue tax** (Polsia's 20% of revenue + ad spend).
- **Why we win here (whole-product fit):** validate-before-build kills the wasted dud (their #1 cost);
  the Glass Box gives **verifiable proof** (their #1 trust fear); **0% cut** keeps their upside;
  human-in-control keeps them the founder.
- **Reachable channel (decisive for Moore):** Indie Hackers, X build community, r/SaaS & r/indiehackers,
  Product Hunt, Hacker News — and, respectfully, the **competitors' own review/complaint threads** where
  the burned segment already gathers.
- **Big enough, small enough:** hundreds of thousands of indie builders; we need only **~45–150 paying**
  for $10K MRR. A niche, not a mass market — exactly right for a beachhead.

### Pressure-test (Moore's "is this a real beachhead?" checklist)
| Test | competitor.inc | Pass? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single identifiable buyer | the multi-bet indie operator | ✅ |
| Compelling reason to buy (must-have) | stop wasting weeks on duds + don't trust black-box builders | ✅ |
| Whole product feasible with our resources | validate + proof + gated execution already built | ✅ |
| Reachable channel | online indie communities + rivals' review threads | ✅ |
| Word-of-mouth / references spread | indies are **vocal** — reviews + threads compound | ✅ (key) |
| Counter-positioned vs incumbents | the autonomous tools are the *anti-pattern* for this segment | ✅ |

**Alternatives considered (and why not first):**
- *All indie founders / "build any company"* → too broad; that's where the funded giants spend. (We
  expand here *after* the beachhead.)
- *Agencies / dev shops* → higher ACV but slower sales + harder to reach cold. Phase 2 expansion.
- *Non-technical creators / SMBs* → big but the most trust-sensitive and hardest to support early.

> Narrow further if we want even sharper: **"indie founders who got burned by Polsia/NanoCorp."** Same
> wedge, hotter intent, fewer people — a great *launch-day* target even if the durable niche is broader.

## 2 · Sharpened positioning (Dunford "Obviously Awesome")

- **Competitive alternatives:** autonomous AI builders (Polsia / NanoCorp / HeyBoss·Tycoon) · "just build
  it" tools · doing it by hand.
- **Our unique attributes (only we combine all four):** validate-before-build · Glass-Box **verifiable**
  proof · human approval on anything consequential · **0% revenue share** (+ own-your-data, public board).
- **The value those enable:** *don't waste months on ideas nobody wants · trust what your AI actually did ·
  keep 100% of your upside · stay the founder.*
- **Who cares most:** the beachhead above.
- **Category we frame:** **"the proof-first AI co-founder"** — the honest counter-category to "autonomous
  company builders."

**Positioning statement**
> For **multi-bet indie founders** tired of building things nobody wants and done with black-box AI
> builders, **competitor.inc is the proof-first AI co-founder** — it **validates demand before it builds,
> shows every action with verifiable proof, asks before it spends, and takes 0%.** Unlike Polsia and the
> autonomous builders, it won't build blind, can't mark work "done" without deploying, and never taxes
> your revenue.

**One-liner:** *Prove it before you build it. Keep 100%.*

**Three proof points (put these on the page):**
1. **"It'll tell you *not* to build."** (the Validation Gate verdict — screen-recorded)
2. **"Every action, with proof."** (the public `/live` board + Glass Box)
3. **"0% revenue share. You keep everything."** (next to Polsia's 20%)

## 3 · The surprise-launch plan (big-bang — borrow audiences, don't build one)

We have **$100 and no audience**, so we **borrow** distribution on drop day (the surprise advantage), and
ride the category's existing *"is this AI-company stuff slop/scam?"* skepticism **toward** us with proof.

**Before (silent):**
- Keep building silently. Stand up a **waitlist** (a waitlist is not build-in-public).
- Tease a **capped Founding offer — $99 once, ~150 seats** (scarcity).
- Prep assets: a **60–90s demo** (the 3D floor + Glass Box proof + the honest "don't build it" verdict),
  the **Show HN** post, the **Product Hunt** page, an **X thread**, and 3–5 community posts.

**The contrarian hook (the megaphone):**
> **"I built an AI co-founder that refuses to build your startup — until it proves people want it."**
Counter-intuitive + honest; it stands out *because* every rival screams "we build it instantly."

**Drop day (coordinated, one day):**
- **Show HN + Product Hunt + X** simultaneously; Founding seats live.
- Lead with **proof, not promises**: the public `/live` board + the **House** ("competitor.inc is run by
  competitor.inc — watch") as the meta-story.
- Seed the **beachhead channels**: Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, the rivals' review threads (fair, not snarky:
  *"burned by tasks-marked-done? here's the proof-first one, 0% cut"*).

**After (compounding):**
- **Surge** drafts demand-capture posts (founder-approved; never an auto public diary).
- Own **one channel**: SEO comparison content — *"honest Polsia alternative," "validate before you build,"
  "AI co-founder with 0% revenue share."* High-intent, compounding, on-brand.

**Pricing at launch:** Validate **$0** · Operator **$39/mo** · Founding **$99 once** (capped) — **no
revenue share**, headline it next to Polsia's 20%. ([`PLAYBOOK-revenue-10k.md`](PLAYBOOK-revenue-10k.md)
for the $10K math.)

**Go / no-go (Levels):** **~$10K MRR by month 2, or kill it.** Same honesty bar we hold the user's ideas to.
