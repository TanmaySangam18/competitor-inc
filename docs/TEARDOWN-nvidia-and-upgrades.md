# Teardown — build.nvidia.com → competitor.inc upgrades

> Studied **build.nvidia.com** (a best-in-class, deep-pocketed developer-product page) inch by inch and
> translated what makes it *feel* winning into concrete upgrades for competitor.inc. Lens = a blend of
> **Refactoring UI** (hierarchy, contrast, spacing, design-with-real-data, one primary action),
> **Nielsen/NN-g heuristics** (recognition over recall, match to the real world, minimalist), **Kano**
> (basic / performance / delight), and **Jobs-to-be-Done** (lead with the user's job, not the pitch).
>
> **Honest framing:** NVIDIA is a different category (a model catalog) with a trillion-dollar polish team —
> we copy *principles*, **not** their dark skin (we deliberately chose Paper & Ink). And polish is
> *necessary, not sufficient*: the thing that decides competitor.inc is still **distribution**, not another
> redesign. So this is a tight, prioritized list — not a rabbit-hole.

## What NVIDIA does well (the patterns)
1. **Action-first hero** — the headline ("Start Building Your AI Here.") sits over a huge **search bar**; the *first thing* you can do is the core job, not read marketing.
2. **Try it instantly, zero setup** — every model has an in-browser playground + a ready-to-paste API snippet. Time-to-wow ≈ seconds, no signup.
3. **Featured spotlight card** — one bold banner for the newest thing ("Introducing NemoClaw").
4. **Capability pills** — tiny tags (`agent`, `moe`, `physical ai`, `+8`) make dense lists instantly scannable.
5. **Quantified credibility** — "153 skills", "24 skills" — concrete numbers build trust (Made to Stick: concrete + credible beats adjectives).
6. **Cost/risk killed up front** — "**Free** inference with leading models" removes the #1 objection immediately.
7. **One obvious CTA per section** — More Models / View Skills / Explore Blueprints / Start Now. Never ambiguous.
8. **Premium polish** — one consistent card system, strong hierarchy (hero → categories → deep), generous spacing, a subtle animated tech-motif background.
9. **Fast discovery** — ⌘K search + a clean, flat top nav.

## Do-now quick wins (freeze-safe, ~high leverage)
**#1–#4** are small, landing-only, copy/layout changes — no backend, no risk, QA stays green. Together they sharpen the **time-to-"aha"** and put our **trust wedge** (0% cut, never-charged, proof-logged) at eye level — the exact funnel levers for the $10K goal. Recommend shipping these as one tight pass.

## The honest verdict
NVIDIA's polish is worth borrowing, but the gap between competitor.inc and "winning" is **not polish — it's people knowing it exists.** Do the four quick wins (they genuinely help conversion), keep the rest as a post-launch backlog, and put the real energy into the **distribution playbook** + the **Monday handoff**. A beautiful product nobody visits still makes $0.
