# VISION — Competitor.inc: 40,000 AI Companies

_The north star. `MISSION.md` is the operational spec; this is the "why" that every decision serves.
When the two ever conflict, this vision wins on intent, MISSION.md wins on mechanics._

Competitor.inc is not another SaaS product. It is the world's first **Autonomous Company Platform**.

> 40,000 people are waiting. They should be able to describe a software company in plain English, click
> Create, and wake up owning a real software business that is built, operated, improved, marketed, sold,
> and supported almost entirely by AI.

Its purpose is not to help people build software. Its purpose is to **build companies** — not websites,
not MVPs, not prototypes. Entire companies. The owner remains the owner; the AI becomes the workforce.

## The core vision
A university with ~40,000 students. Every one has an idea. Almost none become companies, because starting
one demands ~20 specialties (PM, UX, frontend, backend, DevOps, infra, marketing, sales, support, finance,
accounting, analytics, docs, legal prep…). Most ideas die because one human can't be twenty specialists.
Competitor.inc removes that limit: every user gets an AI-operated company that does the work of a whole team.

## The mission — two questions, forever
Across the entire life of a company, the owner answers only:
1. **Approve this plan?**
2. **Approve this payment?**

Everything else runs automatically: discover → validate → research → spec → design → build → test → review →
deploy → monitor → fix → document → support → market → sell → invoice → book → price → optimize → grow —
without human labor.

## Every user owns an isolated Company Instance
Each contains its own: database · codebase · vector memory · secrets vault · audit log · financial ledger ·
agent organization · infrastructure · deployment pipeline. No company's data ever leaks into another.

## Every company has an AI organization
CEO · Product · Engineering (frontend/backend/infra/QA/review) · Design (UX/UI/brand) · Marketing (SEO/social/
email/ads/partnerships) · Sales (leadgen/CRM/outreach) · Support (tickets/docs) · Finance (books/invoices/
forecasting) · Security (compliance/pentest/secrets/monitoring/IR) · Legal-prep. **The AI is never the legal
owner — the human always is.**

## The two gates (permanently human)
- **Gate 1 — work:** nothing significant runs until the AI presents objective · plan · estimated cost ·
  risks · rollback · expected outcome, and the owner approves.
- **Gate 2 — money:** every dollar (infra, subscriptions, refunds, pricing, vendors, ads, purchases) needs
  explicit approval. **Budget caps live outside prompts** — the platform stays safe even if an AI fails.

## Reliability before scale (earn the right)
- **Phase 0:** one company, one founder, one real product, one real customer, one real payment — operated
  30 consecutive days with only Gate 1 + Gate 2 human. Only then continue.
- **Phase 1:** ten companies · **Phase 2:** hundreds · **Phase 3:** thousands · **Final:** 40,000
  simultaneously, without degradation.

## Engineering principles
Behave like a world-class engineering org. Never fake completion, hide failures, or claim success without
evidence. Every feature: tests · review · monitoring · rollback · docs. Every deploy reversible. Every
architecture decision states trade-offs. Every unknown documented as unknown.

## Security · Memory · Self-improvement
- **Security is foundational:** never expose keys/credentials/customer/financial data; secrets only in the
  vault; scan every repo; secret-detect every deploy; log every action.
- **Memory:** each company remembers everything (decisions, architecture, customers, bugs, deployments,
  finances, experiments, lessons). Every session starts by reading company state and ends by updating it.
- **Self-improvement:** measure → analyze → find bottlenecks → propose → implement (approved) → measure →
  repeat. Every mistake becomes institutional knowledge.

## Ultimate success
Someone non-technical says *"I want to build software for fitness coaches"* — and the platform stands up an
autonomous company that researches, validates, designs, builds, deploys, markets, acquires customers,
processes payments, supports users, and improves itself — asking the owner only: **Approve this plan?** and
**Approve this payment?** And 40,000 such companies run in parallel without quality degrading — the world's
first company-of-companies: **operated by AI, owned by humans.**

Every architectural decision, feature, workflow, and subsystem moves toward this — prioritizing correctness,
reliability, safety, and trust over speed or scale.
