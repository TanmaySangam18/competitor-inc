# Cohort-owner outreach — drafts (founder sends; nothing auto-sends)

**Rule:** every claim here is true or clearly a question. No fabricated traction, no "hundreds of
teams," no invented outcomes — the whole pitch is that we don't fake outcomes. Personalize the
`[brackets]` per target from [cohort-targets.md](cohort-targets.md). *Playbooks: The Mom Test
(ask about their world, not our product) + Blond (lead with their problem, not our features).*

**Sending reality (F1):** these are drafts for YOU to send from your own email once you're ready.
Warm/in-person first (NU), email second. Don't blast — 8–10 personalized sends/day.

---

## 1 · Warm — Northeastern (in-person or email)

> Subject: a Husky building a validation tool for IDEA teams — 15 min?
>
> Hi [Name], I'm Tanmay, a Northeastern student. I built competitor.inc — it walks a first-time
> founder through validating an idea (real demand test + honest go/tweak/kill) *before* they spend a
> semester building the wrong thing.
>
> Not asking you to buy anything. I want to learn: when a team in [program] has an idea, how do they
> figure out if it's worth building today — and how do you show your funders which teams actually got
> traction? That second part is what I keep hearing is painful.
>
> Could I show you what I've built for 15 minutes this week? If it's useful I'd love a few IDEA teams
> to try it free and tell me where it's wrong.

## 2 · Cold — accelerator / E-center director (email)

> Subject: outcome evidence for [program]'s cohort — worth a look?
>
> Hi [Name], I run competitor.inc — an AI co-founder that makes each founder *prove real demand before
> building* (customer conversations, a real demand test, a costly-ask). The part I think matters for
> [program]: it produces cohort-level evidence — how many teams validated, how many killed a bad idea
> early, committed pre-orders — the kind of outcome data funders actually want.
>
> Quick question, not a pitch: how does [program] currently capture what came out of a cohort beyond
> demo-day? If that's ever a scramble, I'd love to show you the evidence dashboard for 15 minutes.
>
> Happy to run a free pilot with one cohort so you can judge it on real teams, not slides.

## 3 · The Mom-Test discovery call (not a script to read — a guide)

Open: "Before I show anything — tell me about your last cohort. What did the teams struggle with most?"
Ask about the PAST, never "would you use X":
- "Walk me through what a team does today between 'I have an idea' and 'I'm building it.'"
- "Last time a team built something nobody wanted — what happened? What did it cost them / you?"
- "When your funders ask 'what came of this cohort,' where does that number come from today?"
- "What have you already tried or bought to help teams validate?"
Only after they've talked: show the Validation Gate + the Cohort Lab evidence dashboard.
The ask ladder (end one rung up): free pilot for one cohort → intro to a peer program → a start-date
for a Cohort Lab license.

## 4 · The "killed early is a win" one-liner (use everywhere)

> "Most tools help teams build faster. We help them find out — honestly — whether to build at all. A
> semester saved is the outcome your funders are really paying for, and it's the one number no other
> tool will give you."
