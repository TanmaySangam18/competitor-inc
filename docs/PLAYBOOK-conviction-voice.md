# Playbook — the Conviction Voice (make them feel inevitable, honestly)

> **Goal:** a first-time founder should land on competitor.inc and feel *"I'm actually about to build
> something huge"* — without us ever **promising** an outcome. Energy comes from **possibility + proof**,
> not hype.
> **Playbooks:** **StoryBrand** (the user is the hero, we're the guide) · **Made to Stick** (concrete,
> credible, emotional) · **Cialdini** (confidence + commitment) — all bounded by our **honesty rule**.

---

## The core trick
**Honesty *is* the hype.** "The only AI co-founder honest enough to tell you *no*" is what makes the
*yes* feel real. Conviction without a single false promise — that's the energy money can't fake, and the
funded incumbents (who over-promise) structurally can't copy.

## The 7 rules
1. **The user is the hero; we're the guide.** Write "you," not "we." It's their company, their win.
2. **Present-tense momentum.** "Tonight," "now," "let's go" — not "you could one day."
3. **Concrete beats vague.** Numbers + time + proof: "tested in 24 hours," "every action with proof,"
   "you keep 100%" — never "AI-powered growth."
4. **Earn the energy with proof.** The Validation Gate verdict + the Glass Box are the credibility under
   the confidence. Show, then claim.
5. **Aspiration, anchored in truth.** "Every big company started as one tested idea — let's go find
   yours." Big feeling, true sentence. **Never** "you'll be a billionaire / get rich."
6. **No promised outcomes — ever.** We promise the *process and the proof*, not the result. "We'll prove
   it before you build it" ✅ · "This will make you millions" ❌.
7. **One color, one feeling.** A single electric accent + glass = focused, alive, premium. Restraint
   reads as confidence; rainbow reads as a toy.

## Do / Don't
| Say this (conviction + honest) | Not this (empty hype) |
| --- | --- |
| "Prove your idea tonight." | "Launch your startup in minutes!" |
| "Most ideas die unproven. Yours won't." | "Guaranteed product-market fit." |
| "It'll tell you the truth — even 'not yet'." | "Your idea is amazing!" |
| "Build the winner. Keep 100%." | "Get rich with AI." |
| "You stay the founder. We do the work." | "Sit back while AI makes you money." |

## Where the voice shows up
- **Landing hero** — the headline + the one honest, electric promise.
- **Validation verdict** — confident whether it's *build it* or *not yet* (the "not yet" is delivered
  with respect, not deflation).
- **The "Approve build" moment** — the peak: "This one's real. Let's build it." → the 3D floor lights up.
- **Empty states + emails** — momentum, never nagging.

## The visual half (single-color, glassy)
- **One electric accent** (cyan/teal family, à la the color you liked) on a deep, rich canvas.
- **Glass surfaces** — translucent panels, a lit top edge, the accent used *sparingly* on the one thing
  that matters (the CTA, the verdict). Color = meaning, not decoration.
- Premium, not casual: generous space, confident type, restraint. The energy is in the *words + the one
  glowing color + the proof*, not in clutter.

## The line that holds it together
> **Prove it before you build it. Then build something huge — and keep all of it.**

*Conviction from StoryBrand + Made to Stick + Cialdini; truth from our honesty rule. The energy is real
because the proof is real.*
