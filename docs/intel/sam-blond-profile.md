## His core playbook — "9 sales concepts so many get wrong"
1. **Don't outsource recruiting** — "recruiting is the only thing that matters."
2. **Focus on DEMAND until you have too much** — demand-gen is the bottleneck; don't obsess over mid-funnel conversion.
3. **Do things that stand out** — be memorable, not generic.
4. **Leverage happy customers to generate new ones** — referrals/word-of-mouth as a growth engine.
5. **Distribution is as important as the announcement** — shipping/announcing ≠ done; distribution is the work.
6. **Be prescriptive on how to buy** — customers want to be told the happy path; guide them from day one.
7. **The presumptive close** — assume the sale; reduce friction to "yes."
8. **Meet prospective customers in person** — high-trust, high-conversion.
9. **Obsess over implementation** — onboarding/activation is where revenue is kept.

## Other key lessons
- **Founder-led sales is forever:** close your **first 10–20 customers yourself** before hiring reps; and after you hire a VP of Sales, **don't exit sales.**
- **First sales hire timing:** only when you have non-friends-&-family revenue + a repeatable process. **Don't hire a player-coach** (you get someone mediocre at both). Want a leader → hire a leader; want a rep → hire a rep.
- **Don't add reps in a demand-poor environment** — it spreads thin demand and *lowers* productivity. The right constraint to growth is "not enough reps to work existing demand."
- **Hire raw talent on the ascent** (first-time reps/managers) for talent density.
- **14-day read** on early hires; the "sign the offer, 2-week back-out" trick to de-risk recruiting.
- **Discounting is a tool, not a sin**; **urgency** is the real driver.
- **Sell to the business's priorities, not just the buyer**; messaging that worked last year may not this year.
- ⭐ Did a Lenny's episode **"We replaced our sales team with 20 AI agents"** (with Lemkin/SaaStr) — *directly* our thesis.
