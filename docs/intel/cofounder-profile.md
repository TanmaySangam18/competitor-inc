# Competitor intel — Cofounder (cofounder.co)

A direct namesake competitor: an "AI co-founder" that builds + runs a company with you. Source: their
public **"How to run a company with Cofounder"** guide (Start / Build / Sell / Scale), read 2026-06-27.
This profile is internal — never referenced in our public playbooks (see the playbook-tab-framing rule).

## What they're doing (the read)

1. **Content moat.** A long, opinionated 4-part operator's manual (Start → Build → Sell → Scale), each
   concept ending in a **"Try in Cofounder"** CTA that turns the lesson into a product action. This is SEO
   + authority + onboarding + product tie-in in one asset. It's the same shape as our Playbooks tab — they
   went broader and deeper.
2. **Best-of-breed integrations, not NIH.** They wire **PostHog** (product analytics) and **Fin by
   Intercom** (AI support) rather than building them. Pragmatic; the product's value is the *agent that
   sets them up + acts on them*, not reinventing the tool.
3. **Build-first flow.** Their journey moves fast into "come up with a spec → create a repo → deploy."
   Demand validation exists in "Start" (Evaluate Ideas, Pick a Wedge) but the product centre of gravity is
   *building*. Tellingly, the Scale chapter preaches **"test demand before you build it / sell the
   expansion before you build it"** — but applies that discipline mainly to *expansion*, not the first build.
4. **Auto-acts.** "Cofounder can open a PR and deploy a preview" — it takes consequential action for you.
5. **Scaling loop** = build → sell → observe → support → learn → iterate, backed by analytics + support +
   unit economics + expansion (vertical/horizontal). Solid, conventional, well-written.

## The counter-position (our gold)

- **They validate-before-*scale*; we validate-before-*build*.** Their own Scale chapter says "sell it before
  you build it" — for expansion. competitor.inc applies that to the *whole* journey, from idea zero. Sharpest
  line: *"They tell you to validate before you scale. We make you validate before you build."*
- **They auto-act; we prove + ask.** Their loop opens PRs and deploys for you. Ours adds the **Glass Box**
  (proof for every action) + **Approval Inbox** (consequential moves wait for your yes). Trust is the wedge.
- **Their guide validates our loop.** observe/support/learn/iterate ≈ our Operate layer + `revalidate()` +
  loop-engineering doc. We're not behind on concept; we're differentiated on trust + validate-first + 0% rev
  share + own-everything + private mode.

## Gap → action map (what to pull in, scored against our $10K goal + the launch focus)

**NOW — launch / $10K-aligned, freeze-safe (it's GTM/founder-tooling, not product sprawl):**
- **Structured operator's guide on the Playbooks tab**, but anchored on *validate-first* and in neutral
  user-resource voice. Mirrors their content moat → SEO + inbound (serves $10K). Lead every "Build/Sell/
  Scale" chapter with "prove it first." Playbook why: Tell-a-Story + Levels (ship content) + it's the cheapest
  inbound channel. Trigger to double down: playbook page → waitlist conversion > a few %.
- **Unit-economics readout on the House board** (founder-only): churn, ARPU, LTV, CAC, LTV:CAC, payback —
  fed by the metrics + (gated) billing. This is literally the instrument for proving competitor.inc's own
  $10K MRR. Playbook why: Walling/$10K is the north star; you can't hit a number you can't see.

**LATER — post-launch roadmap (note, don't build now; respect the launch focus):**
- **Analytics-setup as a gated crew capability** (mirror "Cofounder sets up PostHog"): an executor that
  instruments the user's app + the events that matter. Fits our gated-execution pattern.
- **Support-agent capability** (mirror Fin): generate a knowledge base from the product + an approval-gated
  AI support agent that escalates. Becomes a "Support" seat on the crew.
- **Unit-economics as continuous validation** for customers too — extend `revalidate()` from demand to
  economics (are LTV:CAC and payback trending healthy?). On-brand: validation never stops.

## What NOT to copy
- Don't go build-first to match their speed — validate-first IS the moat.
- Don't auto-act without proof/approval — that's their model, not ours.
- Don't NIH analytics/support — wire PostHog/Intercom-class tools; our value is the agent + the proof layer.
