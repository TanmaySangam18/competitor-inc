# Intel: Polsia founder (Ben) on Kevin Rose's podcast — "The Solopreneur Revolution Is Here"

Source: YouTube `OC9yQ4BsCtA` (Kevin Rose show, ep. 1). Transcript captured 2026-07-01. Everything below
is **Ben's own words on camera** — citable, first-hand. Polsia = "Pulsia"/"Poe C"/"Porsha" (transcription
noise); domains pulsar.ai + .com. This is our #1 rival ([polsia-watch.md]).

## What Polsia is (his framing)
- "An AI that builds and runs companies autonomously." Positioning: **"click a button, get a company"**
  (explicitly modeled on Travis Kalanick's "click a button, get a ride"; Ben worked 5y for Travis at Cloud Kitchens).
- Solo founder, **zero employees** — runs on agents + infra partners (Render, Postmark, Meta, a SF "agents
  infrastructure" startup he calls CPM, + Anthropic/OpenAI).
- Sequencing = idea → prototype → market research → mission doc → landing page → **"outreach to 10 people"**
  (first clickable task) → nightly autonomous cycles (product/research/QA/support/marketing).
- Deliberately **walled-garden / "Apple ecosystem"** by design: Twitter only (no IG/TikTok at launch), no
  BYO-agents, no BYO-accounts. Aesthetic modeled on the game *Universal Paperclips*.

## The numbers he stated
- Pricing history: **free → $19/mo → $29 → $49/mo** (repeatedly says "$50 bucks a month"). ~$1/task cost;
  the monthly buys **30 days of autonomy** + infra + on-demand tasks. Wants to keep it cheap for reach.
- `/live` dashboard: **$4.2M "annual run rate", +35% (later +55%) week-over-week**, ~3,820 active companies,
  353K human messages, 137K tasks, 102K emails.
- **Revenue-share vision = "1% of your revenue" as an aspirational "investor-Polsia"** (NOT built yet).
  ⚠️ Reconcile with the earlier Product Hunt "we take 20% on revenue" quote — the model is evolving/unclear;
  don't cite a single fixed number as current.

## THE CRACKS — admitted on camera (our ammunition, in his words)
1. **"People were paying and it wasn't delivering."** A traffic surge broke it: downtime, DB overflow,
   vendor rate limits, **Meta ads not delivering while customers paid.** He spent ~2 days refunding people
   and bolting on a support agent to "make it right." → *The trust/reliability failure is real and self-reported.*
2. **`/live` is an admitted "marketing stunt"** and the $4.2M is an **annualized run-rate** off a few weeks —
   "most companies are 2–3 weeks old," "we're very early," "not there yet in crazy success stories in terms
   of revenue." → *Vanity/annualized metric, not proven revenue.*
3. **Governance was bolted on, not designed in.** He built the full auto-deploy loop, then **PAUSED it**
   because shipping to prod unattended is "very scary"; agents now Slack him for ad-hoc yes/no. → *He is
   retrofitting exactly what competitor.inc has by design: Approval Inbox + policy engine + Glass Box.*
4. **No governance surface for the customer** — approvals are his private Slack, not a product feature.
5. **Walled garden = "limiting" for anyone technical** (his word). No BYOK, no own accounts, no own agents.
6. **Single point of failure**: "people don't care that you're a one-person company." Support, infra, refunds
   all bottleneck on Ben.
7. **No ICP / GTM by choice**: "I stopped caring about who's your ICP and what's your go-to-market" (built
   for himself, Rick Rubin creativity). Marketing = Twitter preaching + Meta AI-video ads + cold email.

## What he gets RIGHT (respect / learn, don't dismiss)
- The **free onboarding "oh moment"** (instant build, reads your name) — costs him ~$2–4/user, works as a
  wake-up call even without conversion.
- **Co-founder-not-assistant** prompting that pushes back ("you have zero customers — talk to 5 friends
  first") — same honest-validation spirit as ours.
- Distinctive anti-generic design; sharp one-liner; loud solopreneur mission on Twitter.
- **Partner-not-hire** infra strategy; "smallest teams win in 2026"; "80% autonomous to survive."
