## 2026-06-20 (baseline)

**Positioning / headline:** "AI That Runs Your Company While You Sleep" — autonomy-forward, "CEO agent"
that runs continuously *without* human involvement.

**Pricing (changed from our older notes):** Free + **Starter $49/mo** + **Pro $99/mo** + Enterprise.
The $49 now bundles **one daily autonomous task + 5 monthly on-demand credits (10 bonus first month) +
a ~$5/mo API allocation**. **Revenue share: 20% of business revenue once profitable.** → This confirms
two things we'd modeled: (1) the subscription is calibrated to roughly cover compute (they literally
bundle ~$5 of API), and (2) the "hit the limit → pay to continue" credit wall is real.

**Scale (CORRECTION):** Third-party trackers now report **~700 companies, $450k+ ARR, launched ~4 months
ago.** This is *far smaller* than the "~$10M ARR / 7.6k customers" figure in our older competitive docs
(which itself was flagged "varies by source"). Treat **$450k ARR / 700 companies / ~4 months old** as the
credible current number. **Implication: the gap to catch them is much smaller than we feared.**

**New capabilities:** persistent agent **memory threads**, **MCP integrations** for live data, and
autonomous **investor-relations** (managing the founder's inbox, drafting VC replies, fundraising comms —
the founder "gave the AI his inbox for 14 days"). They're chasing flashy autonomy demos.

**Trust signals (our wedge — confirmed live):** public complaints — one user "withdrew my project before
it goes too far without my control," another said the AI "made unauthorized commitments." Founder's
defense is **reactive**: "you can see everything in the dashboard," "own your data, export anytime," "you
can always jump in and correct it."

**Top 3 takeaways:**
1. **Their #1 weakness is still control** — and their answer is "correct it *after*." Our Approval Inbox
   answers *before* the action. This is the sharpest wedge; lead every message with it.
2. **They're going upmarket + flashy** (Pro $99, Enterprise, AI-negotiates-with-VCs). Leaves the
   trust-first, validate-first, *burned-indie* lane wide open — our beachhead.
3. **They're smaller and younger than assumed** ($450k ARR, ~4 mo). The race is winnable; the bottleneck
   remains distribution, not their lead.
