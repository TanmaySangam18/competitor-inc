# Warm outreach (DMs)

DMs convert higher than any feed post. Keep them **personal, honest, and few** — reference the
person's actual words, never blast a copy-paste to strangers.

## Who to reach (warmest first)
1. **People who got burned by an AI-builder tool** — find them in 1-star reviews (Trustpilot,
   review sites) and in "is X a scam / is it legit / it built junk" threads on X, Reddit
   (r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur, r/SideProject), and Hacker News. They have the exact pain we fix.
2. **Indie hackers validating side-projects** — Indie Hackers, relevant Discords, build-in-public X.
3. **Your own network** — anyone who's mentioned an idea they're "thinking about building."

## Templates (personalize the first line every time)

**A — the burned-by-an-AI-builder angle**
> Hey [name] — saw your note about [their specific complaint, e.g. "it burned credits on a build
> that never shipped"]. That's exactly the trap I built competitor.inc to avoid: it validates demand
> *before* it builds anything, and shows its work. Want a free Founding seat to try it on an idea?

**B — the indie validator angle**
> Hey [name] — you mentioned wanting to test [their idea]. competitor.inc runs a real demand check
> (landing page + waitlist + a small ad test) and gives you an honest go/kill before you build.
> Free to try — would love your read on whether the signal feels real. [link]

**C — your network**
> [name] — finally shipping the thing I've been building: an AI that pressure-tests a startup idea
> before you build it (and tells you when not to). You've got [their idea] kicking around — want to
> run it through? [link]

## Rules
- One genuine, specific opener per person. If you wouldn't send it to a friend, don't send it.
- Lead with *their* problem, not our features.
- Offer the free validation or a Founding seat — give before you ask.
- No mass DMs, no scraping, no unsolicited blasts. Quality > volume.
