# competitor.inc — Launch Kit

Everything to put competitor.inc in front of the world on **launch day (June 28)**. Most of this is
copy you (or your techie friend) just **publish**. Replace `[@yourhandle]` everywhere with your real
handle before posting.

## Launch-day sequence (≈ in this order)
1. **Deploy is live** (see `runbook.md`) and the domain resolves.
2. **08:00 — Product Hunt** goes up (`product-hunt.md`) — schedule the night before.
3. **08:05 — Show HN** post (`show-hn.md`).
4. **08:10 — X launch thread** (`x-thread.md`), pinned.
5. **All day — DMs** to the warm list (`dm-script.md`) — the highest-converting channel.
6. **Reply to everything.** Be present in the HN/PH/X comments — founder presence is the multiplier.

## The contrarian hook (the spine of all copy)
> Everyone's shipping "AI that builds your startup." competitor.inc is the AI that **tells you not to** —
> until it's proven there's real demand. Then it builds the winner.
