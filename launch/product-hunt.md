# Product Hunt

**Name:** competitor.inc

**Tagline (≤60 chars):**
> The AI co-founder that proves it before it builds it

**Alt taglines:**
> Validate your startup idea before you build the wrong thing
> An AI builder honest enough to say "don't build this"

**Topics:** Artificial Intelligence · SaaS · Developer Tools · No-Code · Marketing

**Description:**
competitor.inc is a validation-first AI co-founder. Describe an idea and it runs real demand
experiments — landing page + waitlist, fake-door test, a small ad smoke-test, and search demand —
then returns an evidence-backed verdict with a confidence score. If the signal's weak, it tells you
to hold. Only when you approve does it build the winner — in the open, in a Glass Box audit log,
with approvals on anything that spends money or sends a message, and one-click undo. BYOK, no
revenue rake, full export, no lock-in. You stay the founder.
